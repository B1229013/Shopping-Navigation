AppNav VLM×OCR Comparison

VLM and OCR are chosen independently per slot. Each slot runs its OCR engine, injects the extracted text into the VLM prompt, then runs its VLM — so you can hold the VLM fixed and compare OCR engines (EasyOCR vs deepseek-ocr vs glm-ocr), or hold OCR fixed and compare VLMs. CGU models use one key + base URL; Ollama is local; EasyOCR is your backend's POST /ocr.
